# Basic design writing guidance

- Put the function name in the title.
- Under **Purpose and scope**, describe the purpose, users, and scope of the function.
- Under **Business rules**, record decision criteria, constraints, and any supported permissions or integrations.
- Under **Inputs and outputs**, describe the input and data constraints and observable outputs.
- Under **Normal behavior** and **Error behavior**, describe the respective preconditions, actions or inputs, and observable results.
- Under **Sources and references**, cite evidence that readers can reach from the canonical design. Mark gaps as unverified in the corresponding field and record what needs checking and why it matters.
